Spotlight: Study & Work in Malta

Updated

We are excited to share opportunities for Work and Study in Malta at selected institutions. Explore programs, eligibility, and application requirements below. T&C applies.

Programs (MQF Levels 5 & 7):

  • Diploma in Information Technology (MQF level 5)
  • Diploma in Tourism and Hospitality Management (MQF level 5 and 7)
  • Diploma in Business Management (MQF Level 5)
  • Diploma in Strategic Management and Leadership (MQF level 7)
  • Diploma in Health and Social Care Management (MQF level 5 and 7)
  • Award MQF level 6 in Nursing and Health Studies (bridging course, 6 months)

Eligibility (MQF Level 5 Diploma):

Senior secondary school certificate, WASSCE, Diploma/Advanced Diploma, or HND.

Documents: copies of results/certificates, passport + photo, contact & emergency info, college application form, signed agency contract.

Eligibility (MQF Level 7 Diploma – Masters equivalent):

Bachelor’s, postgraduate diploma, or master’s holders.

Documents: copies of senior secondary/WASSCE, BSc/postgrad/master’s results, passport + photo, contacts, application form, signed agency contract.

Postgraduate Programs:

  • PG Diploma in Neuroscience for Business — Tuition: €3500
  • MSc in Sports Neuroscience & Human Performance — Tuition: €4000
  • PG Diploma in Neuromarketing & Artificial Intelligence — 30 ECTS — 12 months — Tuition: €3000
  • PG Diploma in Multidisciplinary Applied Neuroscience — 30 ECTS — 12 months — Tuition: €3000

Requirements include WASSCE/senior secondary certificates and HND or Bachelor’s (for PG diplomas/MSc), financial declaration, authorization form, and signed agency contract.

One-year Programs (Discounted Tuition):

  • Master’s in Human Resources Management — €5000 (full tuition due upon visa approval)
  • Undergraduate Diploma in Human Resources Management
  • Undergraduate Diploma in Occupational Health & Safety — €3750
  • Undergraduate Diploma in Digital Marketing — €3750 (full tuition due upon visa approval)
  • Undergraduate Diploma in Business Management — €3750 (full tuition due upon visa approval)

Eligibility:

Undergraduate diplomas: Senior secondary/WASSCE/Diploma/Advanced Diploma/HND. Master’s HR: Senior secondary/WASSCE and Bachelor’s results/certificates.
